FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Shraga Funding a direct lender or a broker?
We're a direct lender. We fund with our own capital and underwrite in-house, so the team that approves your deal is the team that writes the check. You're not being shopped to a network of third parties.
Will applying hurt my credit?
No. Our initial application uses a soft credit pull only, which never impacts your score. A hard pull happens only later, with your consent, if you move forward with a formal offer.
How fast can I actually get funded?
Most applicants receive a decision within 24 hours. Working capital and term loans can fund in as little as one business day; construction and real estate facilities fund on a draw or closing schedule set at term sheet.
What types of projects and businesses do you finance?
We finance construction and commercial real estate, plus general business needs, term loans, lines of credit, equipment, and working capital, across most industries. If you have revenue or a fundable project, we want to hear about it.
How much can I borrow?
Facilities range from $10,000 for working capital up to $10M+ for construction and commercial real estate. The amount depends on your project, revenue, collateral, and use of funds.
What documents do I need to apply?
To start, just the basics: business details, recent bank statements, and an overview of your funding need. For construction and real estate, we'll also request project plans, a budget, and any relevant appraisals or contracts.
